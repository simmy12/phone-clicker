Questions

Does it really work on a kosher phone?

Yes. Players only make a regular phone call and press keys. Any phone that can call a US number works, with no internet and no apps.

Does the call cost the players money?

It's a regular US number, like any local or long-distance call on a normal US phone plan.

What do I need at home?

One screen everyone can see (TV, laptop or computer) with internet, and a phone for each player. All players sit together in the same room.

Can we play more than once?

Your game link plays one full game. The practice link can be used as many times as you like.

Who writes the questions?

Questions are about Chanukah halacha, history and minhagim, written for frum families.
